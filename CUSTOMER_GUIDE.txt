Celebrity Fitness Manager — Quick Start

1. Double-click GymManagerSetup.exe
2. Click Install
3. Double-click "Celebrity Fitness Manager" on your desktop
   (or run start_gym.bat in the install folder if the shortcut fails)
4. Your browser opens automatically — log in: admin / admin123
5. Start adding members!

YOUR DATA STAYS ON THIS PC
  Database:  %APPDATA%\GymManager\data\gym.db
  Photos:    %APPDATA%\GymManager\uploads\members\
  No internet or cloud account needed.

ONE PC ONLY (owner's computer)
  Use the browser on the same PC after launching the app.
  Address: http://127.0.0.1:8000

STAFF ON SAME WiFi (free local network)
  After launching on the gym PC, the dashboard shows a link like:
    http://192.168.x.x:8000
  Staff on phones/laptops connected to the SAME gym WiFi can open that
  address in any browser and log in (staff / staff123).
  IMPORTANT: Use only on your trusted gym WiFi. Do not expose to the public internet.

TROUBLESHOOTING
  - "Port 8000 already in use" — the app may already be running; check your browser.
    Close any other copy, or restart the PC.
  - Browser did not open — go to http://127.0.0.1:8000 manually.
  - Staff cannot connect — confirm they are on the same WiFi as the gym PC and
    Windows Firewall allows the app on private networks (first launch may prompt).
